01

branding

make the brand easier to understand, remember and trust.

i shape visual identity, messaging and positioning, from identity systems to playbooks and positioning frameworks. the goal is a brand that knows what it stands for and can express it clearly.

02

website development

turn attention into a website that makes people act.

i design and build clean, fast, responsive websites and landing pages that make the value obvious and the next step easy. from portfolios to product pages, the focus stays on clarity, speed and action.

03

social media

build an attention engine, not a posting schedule.

i develop short-form ideas, hooks, scripts and social-first content systems built around how people actually consume content. the aim is consistent distribution that earns attention, builds trust and gives the brand something worth following.

04

content marketing

turn expertise into content people find, read and remember.

i write essays, case studies, newsletters and editorial content backed by search strategy and real research. the result is content that can rank, get shared and build authority.

05

pitch decks & presentations

make complex ideas easy to believe.

i structure and design high-stakes decks for fundraising, sales, keynotes and boardrooms. i turn numbers, strategy and product detail into clear stories people can understand, remember and act on.
