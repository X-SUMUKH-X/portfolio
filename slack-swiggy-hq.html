culture / campaign

slack is the new swiggy HQ.

slack-first swiggy: a behaviour-shift campaign, not a policy launch.

the campaign idea

all the real swiggy stuff now happens on slack. miss slack, miss out.

every team has 17 whatsapp groups, 4 side DMs, and no single source of truth. work, memes, escalations and dashboards are scattered. slack-first swiggy turns slack into the only place where the company truly exists: decisions, inside jokes, launches, leader hot-takes and city wins. whatsapp becomes what it was always meant for: family, friends and alumni groups.

80%employees daily active on slack
90%core work conversations in slack

behaviour design

#inside-swiggy — the no-screenshot club

a single always-on insider channel where the most exciting, messy, unpolished stuff lives. product mocks, unreleased ad films, crazy customer stories, WIP dashboards, viral tweets about swiggy — all drop here first.

slack-only standups

each pod/team gets one fixed slack channel for updates: yesterday / today / blocked on. the goal is speed without chaos.

#swiggy-scoreboard

real-time milestones, shoutouts and a weekly leaderboard. status comes from showing up where the work lives.

#chai-biscuits — meme fridays

theme-based memes, screenshots and reels inside one shared cultural space.

#need-help

one obvious route to answers, backed by slack champions with a <10-minute response promise during work hours.

work = slack. whatsapp = noise.

hero launch asset

the poster is the campaign anchor: a monkey at a desk, locked into a slack screen, surrounded by caffeine and chaos. the Slack screen shows #launch-war-room, #wins and #memes — the reasons people open Slack: ship, flex, laugh.

the orange plush + warm gradient background nod to Swiggy’s energy. the line at the bottom flips the narrative: HQ is wherever the real work, jokes and launches live.

7-day rollout

  1. day 1 — flip the switch. announce the campaign and establish the core channels.
  2. day 2 — first inside drop. put something genuinely exciting only in #inside-swiggy.
  3. day 3 — standups move home. make Slack the default place for team updates.
  4. day 4 — scoreboard & shoutouts. publish milestones + people who helped.
  5. day 5 — meme friday. establish the culture channel and a visible reward.
  6. day 6 — leadership AMAs. make Slack feel like a live room, not an archive.
  7. day 7 — recap & receipts. show the before/after.

strategy + genAI

the campaign doesn’t ask people to “please use slack more.” it rewires incentives. attention goes wherever the fastest answers, juiciest updates and highest status live. put all of that on Slack and use FOMO + identity instead of policy.

ChatGPT / Dia handle the Slack copy. DALL·E generated the hero poster, with prompts refined through ChatGPT. tools like Midjourney or Canva can spin out the rest of the system quickly.
